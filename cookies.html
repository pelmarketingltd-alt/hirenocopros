Cookie Notice

A clear explanation of the cookies we use and how to control them.

Last updated: April 2026

📌 Quick summary: We use cookies to make the site work, understand how it's used, and improve your experience. You can control or block cookies in your browser at any time.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help sites function, remember preferences, and gather information about how the site is used.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use:

  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage statistics (privacy policy)
  • Cloudflare — security and performance
  • GitHub Pages — hosting infrastructure

4. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through:

Your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Our cookie banner:

When you first visit, you'll see a banner letting you accept all, reject non-essential, or customise your choices. You can change preferences at any time by clearing the cookie_consent cookie.

Opt-out tools:

  • Google Analytics opt-out
  • YourOnlineChoices.com (EU)
  • NAI opt-out (US)
⚠️ Note: Disabling strictly necessary cookies will affect site functionality. Some features may not work properly.

5. Do Not Track

We respect Do Not Track (DNT) browser signals where technically feasible. When DNT is enabled, we do not load non-essential analytics or marketing cookies.

6. Changes to This Notice

We may update this Cookie Notice as our practices change. The "Last updated" date will reflect the most recent version.
