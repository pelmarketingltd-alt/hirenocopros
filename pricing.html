Frequently Asked Questions

Are there really no fees for businesses?

Correct. Businesses can search the entire directory, contact any builder, and conduct projects with zero fees from us. We make money from optional builder upgrades and affiliate partnerships with no-code platforms.

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Yes — change your plan any time. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at your next billing date.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — Premium and Top Spot tiers come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.

What's your refund policy?

14-day money-back guarantee on all subscriptions. Verified Pro one-time fees are non-refundable once verification is complete.

Do you take a commission on projects?

Never. When you connect with a builder via HireNocoPro, you handle payment directly with no platform fees, commissions or markups.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. All payments are processed securely through Stripe.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — cancel from your account dashboard with one click. Your premium features remain active until the end of your current billing period.

Are prices in GBP or USD?

Prices are listed in GBP (£). USD and other currency conversions are handled at checkout based on your location.

Do you offer agency / team plans?

Yes — for agencies wanting to list multiple builders, contact partnerships@hirenocopro.com for custom pricing.

Ready to grow your no-code business?
