05 — USAGE RULES

Do's and don'ts

Keep the brand consistent. These rules ensure HireNocoPro is always recognisable and professional.

✓ DO
  • Use the orange "Pro" — it's our signature emphasis
  • Maintain at least 16px of clear space around the logo
  • Use SVG version online for crispness at any size
  • Keep the orange-to-coral gradient direction consistent
  • Pair Fraunces (headlines) with DM Sans (body)
  • Use the navy logo on light backgrounds, white on dark
  • Apply our cream (#FDFAF4) as the default background
✗ DON'T
  • Don't change the colour of "Pro" — orange is sacred
  • Don't stretch, skew, or distort the logo
  • Don't add drop shadows, outlines, or 3D effects
  • Don't place the logo on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Don't substitute Fraunces with other serifs (Times, etc.)
  • Don't use the wordmark and monogram together side by side
  • Don't recolour the favicon — keep it solid orange
